Agent Design Logic

Presentation ready
  1. Read user memory: goals, time horizon, risk tolerance, learning progress, and past emotions.
  2. Identify the current situation: beginner support, market volatility, performance chasing, or monthly review.
  3. Choose a companion action: ask follow-up questions, explain concepts, remind about risk, provide emotional support, or review growth.
  4. Apply safety guardrails: no fund recommendations, promised returns, market predictions, or encouragement to trade.

Safety Guardrails

This demo does not recommend specific funds, predict returns, or provide personalized investment advice. It demonstrates how AI can help young users understand risk, regulate emotions, and keep growing.
